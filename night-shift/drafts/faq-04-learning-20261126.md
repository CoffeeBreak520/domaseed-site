## English candidate

### Is DOMASEED designed as a game or a set of lessons?
The design starts with a game: exploration, discovery and meaningful actions in a connected future world. Learning is intended to arise through what players want to understand and do, rather than through compulsory lessons.

### What does “learning through meaningful actions” mean here?
It describes the design intention that knowledge should connect with a player's reasons for acting in the world. It is not evidence of a completed learning system or a measured improvement in any particular skill.

### Are the four regions already finished learning modules?
No. Deep Blue, Meriden, Aurora and Origin describe the intended connected world. Their presence in the design does not mean four finished regions or a tested curriculum are available.

### What role is AI intended to have?
The stated principle is that AI supports human judgment. The website does not establish a complete list of implemented AI features. Parents should distinguish that principle from claims about a released experience.

### Has DOMASEED demonstrated educational outcomes?
This draft does not claim demonstrated educational outcomes. DOMASEED is still in development. Describing a learning intention is different from providing evidence that it works.

## 中文对应稿

### DOMASEED 是游戏，还是一组课程？
设计从游戏出发：在相互连接的未来世界中探索、发现和进行有意义的行动，希望学习与玩家想了解、想完成的事情相连，而非通过强制课程展开。

### “通过有意义的行动学习”在这里是什么意思？
它描述一种设计意图：知识应与玩家在世界中行动的原因有关。这不是已经完成学习系统或已测得某种能力提升的证据。

### 四个区域已经是完成的学习模块了吗？
不是。Deep Blue、Meriden、Aurora 和 Origin 描述的是设计中的相连世界，不表示四个完整区域或经过验证的课程已经提供。

### AI 的预期角色是什么？
已有原则是让AI支持人的判断。官网没有据此证明完整的已实现AI功能列表。家长需要把设计原则与正式产品体验区分开。

### DOMASEED 已经证明教育效果了吗？
本稿不声称已证明教育效果。项目仍在开发中。说明学习目标与提供有效性证据是两件事。
