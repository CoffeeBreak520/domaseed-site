### An adventure game in development
DOMASEED is an educational adventure game being developed by Optiverse in New Zealand. The design explores a connected future world. The early focus is families with children around ages 10–13.

Deep Blue, Meriden, Aurora and Origin are the four regions in the design. They describe the intended world, rather than a claim that four complete regions are available to play.
