DOMASEED Development Status: Design, Availability and Next Steps

What is DOMASEED?

DOMASEED is an educational adventure game in development by Optiverse (New Zealand). It is being designed for young players, with an early focus on families with children around ages 10–13. This focus is not a formal age rating.

The project has not been publicly released. No public download is announced here.

The design direction

The intended setting is a connected future world with four regions: Deep Blue, Meriden, Aurora and Origin. Exploration, discovery and meaningful actions are central to the design direction.

The aim is for learning to have a context in what players want to do. Discovery, immersion and fun also matter in their own right. This describes the design, not four finished regions or validated educational results.

AI is intended to support human judgment as a tool within the design. This is not an announcement of a released child-facing AI feature.

How to read future previews

A development image, editor view and phone recording can show different things. Any future preview should identify the build or development stage and the capture context, so families can distinguish what is shown from what is still planned.
