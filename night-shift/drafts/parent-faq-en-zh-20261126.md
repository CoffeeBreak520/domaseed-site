## English candidate

### What is DOMASEED?
DOMASEED is an educational adventure game in development by Optiverse in New Zealand. Its design centres on exploring a connected future world. It has not been publicly released.

### Who is it being designed for?
The early design focus is families with children around ages 10–13. This is a design focus, not a confirmed age rating.

### What is planned for the world?
The design describes four connected regions: Deep Blue, Meriden, Aurora and Origin. They describe the intended world, not four finished, playable regions already available.

### Is it a lesson app or a game?
The design starts with adventure, exploration and player choices. Learning is intended to arise through meaningful actions. This is a design aim, not a claim of demonstrated educational outcomes.

### Can we play or download it now?
DOMASEED is still in development. The current website source has not announced a public release, download, release date, price or final age rating.

### Will it be available on phones?
DOMASEED is being designed mobile-first. Confirmed device requirements and app-store availability should be published when established. Mobile-first does not itself confirm iOS or Android availability.

### What role is AI intended to play?
The stated design principle is that AI supports human judgment. This principle does not establish that a particular AI feature is already implemented.

### Is DOMASEED connected with a seed or farming company?
No. DOMASEED here refers to the educational adventure game being developed by Optiverse in New Zealand. Its official website is https://domaseed.com/.
