## English candidate

### Who is DOMASEED being designed for?
DOMASEED's early design focus is families with children around ages 10–13. It is an educational adventure game in development by Optiverse in New Zealand.

### Does that mean it has a 10+ age rating?
No final age rating has been announced in the current website source. The ages 10–13 describe an early design focus. They should not be read as a certification, a suitability assessment for every child, or confirmation of a finished product.

### What can parents understand from the description today?
The project is being designed around exploration and meaningful actions in a connected future world. These statements explain its direction. They do not establish the final difficulty, reading demands, accessibility features or finished playable content.

### What should be confirmed before choosing it for a child?
When release information becomes available, parents will need confirmed information about the actual experience and its requirements. The current development description does not replace those details. DOMASEED has not been publicly released.
