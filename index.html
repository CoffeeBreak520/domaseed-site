Questions parents ask

What is DOMASEED?

DOMASEED is an in-development educational adventure game from Optiverse, set in a connected future world. It is being designed around one connected civilization made up of four interdependent regions.

What will my child do in it?

Players explore, discover and experiment inside one connected civilization made up of four interdependent regions. The design aims to support varied exploration and meaningful actions.

Is it a learning app?

DOMASEED is designed as a game first. Discovery, immersion and fun matter for their own sake. Learning is meant to come up when it helps players do what they want to do, not through compulsory lessons.

How does it use AI?

In DOMASEED, AI is a tool that supports human judgment. It does not replace it.

Who is it for?

DOMASEED is being designed for young players, with an early focus on families with children around ages 10–13. This site is for parents and families.

When can we play?

DOMASEED is still in development. There is no public release, download, price or age rating yet.

Is this site safe for my family?

This site does not collect information from children. The DOMASEED waitlist is not open on this preview.

Why this game exists

DOMASEED began with an art teacher and parent thinking about how children will grow up in a world being changed by AI. As a teacher, she also knew that schools and education systems cannot change overnight.

She started creating lessons at home, shaped around her child's interests and personality. Even so, interest stayed low. However carefully they were designed, the lessons still felt like lessons.

That led to a different question: what if the starting point was a real game? A game where children can try things, discover their own interests and strengths, and learn the knowledge and skills that help them do what they want to do.

Like most parents, she doesn't want children spending more time on screens than they need. But children are growing up in a world shaped by AI, and avoiding it completely isn't realistic. DOMASEED is an attempt to make some of that screen time more active, and more connected to what children want to explore.

Development status

DOMASEED is still in development. There is no public release, download, price or age rating yet.

The four regions describe the design direction, not finished playable content. Any future previews that show prototype material will be labelled as such.

DOMASEED is being designed mobile-first.

DOMASEED is developed by Optiverse (New Zealand).
